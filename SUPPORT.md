# Support

Short version: **malt is unsupported.**

It's a personal project I build for my own daily use and share in the open. I'm
genuinely glad if it's useful to you, but I can't take on the obligations that
come with a supported product. Please read this before opening an issue.

## What that means

- **No SLA, no guaranteed response.** I may reply to an issue tomorrow, in six
  months, or never. Don't block your workflow on a fix from me.
- **No roadmap commitment.** Features land when they scratch one of my own
  itches. A feature request might be a great idea and still never get built.
- **No promise to merge PRs.** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) — patches
  are welcome, but review may be slow or absent.
- **Breaking changes happen.** I version-bump and keep a [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md),
  but this is software I evolve for myself.

If you need something dependable, the best move is to **fork it**. It's MIT —
the code is yours to maintain on your own terms.

## Where to go

- **Questions, tips, show-and-tell →** [GitHub Discussions](../../discussions).
  This is the right place for "how do I…", "is it possible to…", and sharing
  setups. Other users (and sometimes me) can help. Please search first.
- **Reproducible bugs →** [Issues](../../issues), if they're open. Include your
  OS, malt version (Settings → About), and exact steps. Expect silence to be a
  possible outcome.
- **Security issues →** please report **privately** via GitHub's
  ["Report a vulnerability"](../../security/advisories/new) flow rather than a
  public issue. Don't post exploit details in the open.

## Before you ask: help yourself first

- **In-app Tips** — Settings → Tips. Most of the surface is documented there.
- **Shortcuts** — Settings → Shortcuts lists every keybinding, OS-aware.
- **[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)** — what changed, per release.
- **[README.md](README.md)** — install, build, and the data model.

## A note on your data

malt keeps your notes as plain `.md` files in a folder you choose. Whatever
happens to this project, your notes remain ordinary text files you can open with
anything. The one irreversible thing is **encryption: a lost password means a
lost note — there is no recovery.** Back up important passwords.
